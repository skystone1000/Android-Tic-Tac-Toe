# Privacy Policy — XOXO

**Last updated: 28 August 2026**

XOXO ("the app") is an offline tic-tac-toe game published by the developer of
`com.skystone1000.xoxo`. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Short version

The app collects nothing, sends nothing and has no way to. It requests no Android permissions at
all, including no internet permission, so it is technically incapable of transmitting data off
your device.

## Information the app collects

**None.** The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage information.

There is no account system, no sign-in, no cloud sync and no server. The developer receives no
data about you or your use of the app, and has no means of doing so.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, so the game works between sessions. This data stays on your
device and is readable only by the app:

- **Match history** — for each finished round: the game mode, the result (win, loss or draw), the
  AI difficulty if applicable, and the time it was played.
- **Settings** — sound and haptics toggles, theme preference, default AI difficulty, and board
  theme.
- **Display name** — a name you type yourself, shown only on your own device.

## Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting and no
third-party SDKs of any kind. No third party receives data from the app because the app sends
data to no one.

The app does not use the Android Advertising ID.

## Device backup

If Android's system backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may include the app's
local data in your own device backup, stored in your personal Google Drive under your Google
account. This is performed by Android, not by the app; the developer has no access to it. You can
control this in your device's system backup settings.

## Deleting your data

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request deletion of. To remove the data stored
on your device, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children. It has no
chat, no user-generated content and no way for users to communicate with each other.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL and the date at the top
will be revised.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact address listed on the app's
Google Play store listing.
