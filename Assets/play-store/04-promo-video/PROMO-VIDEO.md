# Promo video — specs and shot list

Not rendered. Everything below is filmable from the shipped app with a screen recorder; no
invented screens.

## Play Console requirements

- **Delivery:** a YouTube URL only. Play does not host video files.
- The video must be **public or unlisted** — not private, not age-restricted.
- **Monetisation off.** Ads on the video will get the listing rejected.
- **Landscape 16:9.** Portrait video is letterboxed badly in the store player.
- **First 10 seconds must show the app in use.** No logo-only intro card longer than ~1s.
- Must **stand alone without sound** — the store autoplays muted. Every claim on screen must be
  legible as text or obvious from the UI. No voiceover-only information.
- Keep it **30 seconds**. Play truncates attention long before a minute.
- Do not show Play Store branding, download buttons, prices, ratings or "install now" overlays.
- Do not show a screen the app does not have. Re-read `Ground truth` in the brief before adding
  anything.

## 30-second shot list

Record at 1080 × 1920 portrait on the device, then place it in a 1920 × 1080 landscape frame on
the indigo `#4338CA` field with the motif — the same treatment as the store panels. Left third
for the caption, phone recording on the right. Captions in Space Grotesk 700, white, ≤ 5 words.

| # | Time | Shot | On-screen caption |
| - | ---- | ---- | ----------------- |
| 1 | 0:00–0:04 | Home in the light theme. Tap **Hard**, then **Play vs AI**. Card animation lands. | Pick a difficulty |
| 2 | 0:04–0:12 | Full round against Hard, played at real speed. Tap centre, AI answers, keep going. It ends in a **draw** — Hard cannot be beaten. Let the draw state sit for a beat. | Hard never loses |
| 3 | 0:12–0:17 | Back out, open **Pass & Play**. Two quick alternating taps, then X completes a row: the winning line highlights and confetti fires. | Two players, one phone |
| 4 | 0:17–0:22 | Open **Stats**. Win rate and streak tiles read in, the 7-day chart is visible, scroll the recent-matches list one flick. | Your record, kept |
| 5 | 0:22–0:27 | **Settings**. Toggle dark mode — the whole UI crosses over. Then tap through the three board themes: Classic, Midnight, Aurora. | Light, dark, three boards |
| 6 | 0:27–0:30 | Cut to the stacked logo lockup on `#0E0E14` with the tagline. Hold, then end. | Three in a row. Offline. |

## What not to film

- Anything implying online play, matchmaking, friends or accounts.
- Levels, XP, ranks, badges, achievements or a leaderboard — the win streak is the only
  progression number in the app.
- The Profile screen edited to a name that isn't clearly a placeholder.
- The emulator's screen-recording indicator or the three-button navigation row. Record on a
  device with gesture navigation, or crop both out in the edit.

## Capture checklist

- Enable SysUI demo mode first: fixed clock, full battery and wifi, no carrier, no notifications.
- Populate the match history before shot 4 — an empty stats screen sells nothing.
- Set the display name to something plausible and non-personal.
- Record at 60 fps so the tile animations and confetti stay smooth after compression.
